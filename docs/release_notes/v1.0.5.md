**CR Bevel 1.0.5: fixes for polylines and open meshes**

### Fixed
- **Loose points are kept.** 1.0.4 deleted vertices without edges from every mesh, even with Wire Corners off.
- **Polylines stay connected.** A polyline with a branch, or one touching a face's vertex, was cut at that point; after Apply it fell apart. It's now joined back.
- **Clamp Uniform on polylines is one radius.** It used to lower only the corners next to a short piece (like Per Edge). Now one radius holds for the whole object, faces and polylines together.
- **Negative radius** no longer rounds polylines (it already left edges alone).

### Install
Download **`cr_bevel-1.0.5.zip`** below, then in Blender: **Edit > Preferences > Get Extensions**, open the drop-down at the top right, choose **Install from Disk…** and pick the zip. Requires Blender 5.2 LTS or newer.

**Updating:** files made with an older version keep their own copy of the node group. To use the new one in an existing file, delete the old "CR Bevel" node group, then add the modifier again.
